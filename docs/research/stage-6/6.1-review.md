## A defect the review did not find, found during remediation

While applying R2 and R3, the implementing agent checked the two claims those
findings rested on against the installed schema instead of the interface, and
**both were false**:

- `usage_observations` **is** in the installed schema (`project-001.sql:104-110`)
  with nullable tokens, `cost_microunits`, `currency` and `observed`/`estimated`
  provenance. It is written at `internal/supervisor/runner.go:993` and read at
  `internal/core/finalization.go:532`, where it reaches the factual archive.
- `execution_results_v11` **does** carry a per-attempt `summary TEXT NOT NULL`
  and `changed_paths_json`, written at `internal/supervisor/runner.go:1120` and
  read at `internal/supervisor/reconcile.go:167`.

So the two "product gaps" were never product gaps. They are **interface** gaps:
the data is persisted, queryable and implemented, and the interface reads
neither. This is recorded as a third correction, `6.1-R15`, and it is kept in
place rather than edited away, because the method error is the part worth
preserving — a `grep` of the interface for `cost` proved the *view* was missing
and was generalised to the *capability*, which then justified weakening two
requirements.
